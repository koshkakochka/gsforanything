Navigation

					
					

						W/A/S/D move · Q/E up/down · on Quest: left stick moves,
						right stick turns/up-down · right grip cycles modes (incl. off) · a stick over a window scrolls it.
					

				

				

					
Camera

					

						Pick a camera mode (Orbit, Map, Trackball, Fly, First person or Off) in the Controls tab.
						Reset returns to the start view (4 m back at eye height; Orbit circles the scene origin).
					

					
				

				

					
Origin rotation

					

						Turns the whole scene around its origin so it lines up with the view you want.
						Drag the ball to tilt and turn, drag the ring to roll, click an axis end to face it. In VR: point at it, hold grip and turn the controller.
						Hold Shift or tick Snap for 15° steps.

Camera controls

					

						
						
						
						
						
						
					

				

				
				

					OrbitControls, around the scene origin. Left-drag rotate · right-drag or
					Shift/Ctrl/⌘ + left-drag pan · wheel or middle-drag zoom ·
					←↑→↓ pan ·
					Shift/Ctrl/⌘ + arrows rotate.
					Touch: one finger rotates, two fingers zoom and pan.
				

				

					MapControls. Left-drag pan · right-drag or
					Shift/Ctrl/⌘ + left-drag rotate · wheel or middle-drag zoom ·
					←↑→↓ pan ·
					Shift/Ctrl/⌘ + arrows rotate.
					Touch: one finger pans, two fingers zoom and rotate.
				

				

					TrackballControls, around the scene origin. Left-drag rotate · middle-drag or wheel zoom ·
					right-drag pan · hold A + drag rotate, S + drag zoom, D + drag pan.
					Touch: one finger rotates, two fingers zoom and pan.
				

				

					FlyControls. W/S forward/back · A/D left/right ·
					R/F up/down · Q/E roll ·
					↑/↓ pitch · ←/→ yaw · drag the scene to look.
				

				

					PointerLockControls. Click the scene to look around with the mouse, Esc to release ·
					WASD or ↑←↓→ move.
				

				

					On-screen joysticks: left stick moves (up/down forward and back, sideways strafes), right stick looks around.
					
				

				

					Camera input is off. The camera stays where it is until you pick another mode.
				

				

					Keys work unless you are typing in a text field. Reset Camera View (Env tab) returns to the default view.
				

				

				

					
Game controller

					

					

						Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, PlayStation and other USB or Bluetooth pads.
						The sticks drive the selected control: Orbit and Map: right stick orbits, left stick pans
						(Orbit: up/down zooms), LT/RT zoom out/in ·
						First person: left stick walks, right stick looks ·
						Fly: left stick moves, right stick looks, LB/RB down/up ·
						Trackball: sticks not used ·
						Y switches Orbit/First person · View resets the view.
						Press any button after connecting so the browser picks it up.
						Headset controllers in VR are separate and keep their own mapping.
					

				

				

					
VR windows

					

						
						
					

					

						Fixed keeps the windows where they open. Follow headset lets them catch up gently when you turn
						or walk away, staying upright in front of you. Also on the Environment panel in VR
						(Windows: Fixed / Follow head).
					

				

				

					Desktop and phone only. In a headset, the thumbsticks still move you (see Environment → Navigation).

Render

				
Side-by-side VR180-style capture from the current view. Each eye is an equirectangular image covering the field of view you set (default 180°), composed left | right, with an IPD of 6.4 cm. Menus, controllers and other system objects are hidden.

Screenshots download as PNG (JPEG if very large). Recordings use WebM (VP9 or VP8) or MP4, at about 12 fps, so the live scene — and an active VR session — stay smooth. Recording in VR uses a separate renderer and does not end the headset session. On a phone, the file goes to the usual download or share sheet.
